# Brief (phase 0)

The brief captures what only the user knows, in their words, in `BRIEF.md`.

## Before asking

Read the prompt, the files and links the user named, the working directory, saved preferences and memory, and `defaults.md`.

- **Earlier material** (a handoff, a brainstorm, an old brief or deck): the user's original request outranks it. Note each difference under "Differences from earlier material" for checkpoint 1. With only a handoff, first ask for the original request, or confirm the handoff's quote of it.
- **Memory fills gaps; the user settles identity.** The requester may not be the speaker: put who, which team and which venue to the user, or mark them `CONFIRM`.

## Build the asks ledger

Read the request sentence by sentence; write one row per distinct ask, quoted: named moments, named material, content at the depth described, audience outcomes, style (every reference, adjective and example), constraints.
- **Status:** planned · met · changed (with the user's OK and the date); the row keeps the history. A correction from the user updates the ledger at once.
- **Loose asks** (ones the user hedges) stay in the plan and are the first place to offer an alternative at checkpoint 1.
- **An open brief** (the user asks for ideas) invites better execution and new ideas beside the asks; named moments stay until the user trades them.
- **Material described but unnamed:** find the two or three likeliest candidates and ask which in round 1, thumbnails attached, your pick first.
- **Each image the user attaches or promises:** confirm its use (style reference, or material for a named beat) in round 1.

## How to ask

- At most two rounds. Each asks every open question you can ask now, each with your recommended answer.
- Costliest first: rows 2, 7 and 8, where a wrong guess gets built and rebuilt.
  - Ask for two or three style reference images even when the prompt describes the look in words: one description fits many different looks. When the user has none, offer concrete references.
  - When the user names a brand or institution, ask whether its identity rules apply.
- Use the question tool when available: at most four questions per call, recommended option first, a preview where an option needs detail, room for the user's own words. Otherwise, number them in one message.
- When no human can answer, take the recommended answers and list them under **Assumptions** (SKILL.md, "Autonomy"). When a short answer could also restrict something else, take the reading that restricts least and list it there.

## What to settle

| # | Decision | Default when unanswered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Speaker**: who (with several, who takes which part), first language, delivery, command of the material, how they want to come across, any recording | The requester, in their own register; a recording sets rate and voice (research.md, "Studying exemplars") |
| 2 | **Content source**: research, the speaker's stories and knowledge, their files, or a mix | Research plus the files they named; collect the speaker's stories in their words now |
| 3 | **Audience**: who, how many, what they know, why they came | Inferred from the prompt, and stated |
| 4 | **Purpose**: a decision, understanding, momentum, or a mix | What the kind of talk implies; draft the throughline |
| 5 | **Length and slot**: the hard stop; Q&A inside or after | The length given, Q&A after (directing.md §6) |
| 6 | **Venue and setup**: the place (the talk may use it), screen and aspect, room depth (last row's distance in screen heights), light, noise, network, machine, pointer | 16:9 projector, lit quiet room, network, depth from room size; house defaults for the rest (confirm machine and pointer) |
| 7 | **Style reference**: two or three images, or a Pinterest board link; how lively and how colourful | Your references, shown at checkpoint 2. Lectures, explainers and keynotes default to lively and colourful |
| 8 | **Fixed by the user**: template, palette, aspect, medium, brand rules, must-includes, sensitive topics, language | The prompt's decisions and language; real footage welcome |
| 9 | **Register**: intimate, standard, or stage production | Inferred from purpose and room |
| 10 | **Questions**: at the end, live interruptions, or none | At the end, on the questions beat (directing.md §11) |
| 11 | **Deadline** | Open |
| 12 | **Private material** (the default link is public, unlisted): private people's material, confidential work, the speaker's own accounts on screen | Public material and demo accounts; private material waits for the user's OK |
| 13 | **House defaults and permissions**: every `defaults.md` slot row 6 leaves open; the standing permissions (SKILL.md, "Standing rules"), publishing included | As written, in one question |

## Writing it down

1. Fill `BRIEF.md` from the template, with the request and answers verbatim; paste an answer block as given.
2. Start the **Persistent objects and vocabulary** section of `beats.md`: one meaning per term.
